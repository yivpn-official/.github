**Private Zero-Logs VPN for Windows, macOS & Android** · *iOS Coming Soon*

- **Modern Encrypted Transport** — Built on `sing-box 1.14.3` (`Hysteria2` QUIC & `VLESS+REALITY` TLS 1.3) with volatile RAM-only packet forwarding.
- **Smart Split Tunneling** — Keep local banking, LAN devices, and domestic apps at native direct ISP speed while encrypting external traffic.
